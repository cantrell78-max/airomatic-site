### Headline take

The August agent tape is less “another chatbot” and more **specialized production surfaces**: **agent observability** (what did the fleet actually do), **robotics debug** for physical fleets, **design/engineering agents** for mech-electrical systems, **marketplace ops** automation, **enterprise implementation** agents that finish SaaS rollouts, and **travel booking repair** when AI search breaks PNRs.
