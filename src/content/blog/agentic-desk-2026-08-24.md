## Market research brief (real market — not parody)

**Window:** week of Aug 17–24, 2026 (with Aug 1–15 agent-tracker carry) · **Sources:** Gravity Q3 agent-funding tracker, TechCrunch, CTech / Calcalist, company blogs and PR wires, Crunchbase weekly mega-round tape, D-Wave / CHIPS coverage

### Headline take

August’s disclosed **agent** tape is not “more copilots.” Cheque size roughly doubled versus July (~\$97M average vs ~\$49M) while deal count held. Most of the money still buys the **layer around the agent**: governance (what it may touch), review (what it wrote), runtime (where it runs), context (what it is allowed to know). The one large vertical that already has named customers is **freight ops voice/email**. Around that sit a \$2B voice-interface mark, employee **digital twins**, orbital data centers as a compute region, and a non-dilutive CHIPS-shaped quantum/semi check.
