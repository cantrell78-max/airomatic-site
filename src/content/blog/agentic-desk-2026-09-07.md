## Market research brief (real market — not parody)

**Window:** week of Sep 1–7, 2026 · **Sources:** TechCrunch, Calcalist Tech / CTech, Gravity Q3 agent tracker, Crunchbase News, The Quantum Insider

### Headline take

The first week of September is a split tape. On the US agent-platform trackers, **no confirmed application-agent round has a September announcement date yet** — August closed at seven disclosed agent rounds and ~\$681.5M, and Q3 sits at twenty rounds / ~\$1.32B with September still open. On the actual wires, **governance of what agents install** printed immediately: AIR came out of stealth on Sep 1 with \$50M across two seeds to continuously re-vet skills, plugins, and MCP servers. The loud capital, meanwhile, is **Israeli megadeals and AI infrastructure**, not another Instinct-class assistant: CTech timed ~\$1.16B in the first two days of September, two-thirds of it Wonderful and Upwind. Cheques around the agent (control, compute, physical sensing) are still larger than cheques *for* the agent.
