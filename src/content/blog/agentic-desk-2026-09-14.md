## Market research brief (real market — not parody)

**Window:** week of Sep 8–14, 2026 · **Sources:** TechCrunch, Crunchbase News, company posts (Lightfield, Xapien), SiliconANGLE, Tech.eu, The Quantum Insider

### Headline take

The second week of September is not an “agent round” drought. It is a **systems-of-record and physical-ops week** with legal and sovereign-lab megacheques in the background. Lightfield raised a \$47M Series A to replace the CRM for companies that already run on agents. Superhuman bought YC-backed notetaker Fathom rather than finish one. CloudNC added a \$20M B extension to move from CAM toolpaths into quoting. Xapien took \$56M to make due diligence continuous. Sequoia doubled down on Cymphony to graph what employees *and* agents can reach. Listen Labs walked a signed \$125M term sheet amid reported ~\$2B Salesforce talks. Around that, Cognition’s \$2B / \$48B print and Motive’s \$1.3B physical-ops cheque show where the large dollars still sit: coding agents and fleets, not another household assistant.
