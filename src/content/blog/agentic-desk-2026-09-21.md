## Market research brief (real market — not parody)

**Window:** week of Sep 14–21, 2026 · **Sources:** Crunchbase News, Gravity’s Q3 agent tracker (updated Sep 21), CTech, Reuters, Temporal and Factory company posts, The Quantum Insider, RIABiz

### Headline take

The week after Cognition’s mega-round did not go quiet. It moved from “one coding agent” to the machinery around the agent. [Temporal](https://temporal.io/blog/temporal-raises-usd550m-series-e-at-usd12-55b-valuation-ai) raised \$550M at a \$12.55B valuation for durable execution: a run that can last days and resume after a crash. [Factory](https://factory.com/news/5-billion-valuation) raised \$200M at \$5B and described the shift from individual coding agents to software factories an engineering org can govern. [Noetive](https://www.calcalistech.com/ctechnews/article/hjji2potfx) left stealth at five months old with a \$41M seed for AI outside the screen. Under that, the control layer kept specializing — certify and insure, post into a bank core, let two agents negotiate a hire — while quantum dollars this week were grants and a SPAC-adjacent note, not another hardware Series A.

### Vertical heat map

- **Hot:** Durable execution for long-running agents, software factories, physical-economy systems outside the browser, agent certification and insurance, banking ops that post into the core, two-sided hiring agents, AI answer-engine marketing.
- **Loud but adjacent:** Cognition’s coding mega still sets the ceiling Factory is pricing under. Ridgeline is the allocator cheque. Open-weight models (Arcee), AI networking (Cornelis), and space vehicles (Impulse) are the rest of the top ten.
- **Non-dilutive / not a round:** GlobalFoundries’ CHIPS award, DOE’s Quantum Genesis Q competition, EigenQ’s pre-listing note, Mariner’s per-bot services contract.
- **Cooling here on purpose:** agent-native CRM, meeting-intel M&A, voice customer research, CNC quote-to-chip, continuous diligence, humanoid motion data, silicon identity, clinic front office, jobsites, pure SOC hunt, quantum hardware. Those were the last two batches.
