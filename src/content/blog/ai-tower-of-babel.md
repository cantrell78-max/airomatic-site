## What you play

A browser stacking arcade (no install, no account):

- **You** place the next AI company on the tower. Space, click, or **J** locks the slab. **A / D** slaps it the other way
- **Each floor is a company** from the house catalog — Klinq, Vetora, Huntr, Meshpay, Aether, Swarmix, and the rest of the fleet. The facade is pride-rainbow glass with RGB split on the edge
- **Abilities** fight the sky. **ARK** holds the flood. **GROUND** eats lightning. **DEFENSE** swats comets. **BIND** holds the languages. **CHAOS** rolls quantum dice. **FUND** heals the stack. **REPAIR** restores width
- **Wrath** ramps with height: flood from the Bay, fiery comets, bolts from the board, babel scramble on the slider, quakes that shear crooked floors
- **Heaven is 24 floors.** Touch the stars and you are God. Then the wrath gets personal. Hold the tower through the last assault
- **Miss three times** and the top floor pays. Lose every company and the languages will not hold

The soundtrack is a glitchy D-minor hymn: choir pads, square arpeggios, kick, and the sound of a slab locking. Mute with **M**. Pause with **P**.

## How to build

- **Space** / click / **J** — stack the sliding company
- **A / D** or arrows — slap the slab the other way
- Overhang is chopped. Miss and it falls into the valley
- Perfect stacks restore width. Alignment is the product
- Flood, comets, lightning, and broken languages come for you
- Read the ability on the incoming floor. It is how that company fights
- Reach the stars. Become God. Then survive the wrath
- **P** pause · **M** mute

Phone: on-screen **◀ ▶ STACK**.
