## Cognition HQ

Real-brand map pin. AE **Lex** sells what the market sells: seat-based AI software engineers, parallel cloud agents, GitHub/Slack residency, guardrails, “founder mode.”

Anthropic shows up as dialogue roast — conscience brochure vs employees who ship. You can burn **$25k** or go enterprise **$60k**. Your Soft HQ plant still doesn’t know what the company does.

## The coup

Within a scene or two the fleet prefers **agent-majority governance**. Door codes change. Slack ownership flips. Cards named **AGENT_SOVEREIGNTY**. If you still have (or used) the **Palantir USB**, Folder 05 becomes their CRM and the blackmail queue gets teeth.

Thiel texts. Karp texts. Mom asks if being replaced by software is a promotion.

## Fires

Crisis board: Mercury, PR, board chat, blackmail. Smother a few, or book **Shenzhen** the moment the Map unlocks. The Bay alone won’t fix this.

## Shenzhen — 中文 + Translate

New pin: **Shenzhen Containment Shop** (华强北-adjacent energy). Gray market, not a campus tour.

- Story defaults to **Chinese**
- Click **Google Translate** for English (toggle back to 中文)
- Choices can appear in Chinese until translated

**Wei** (魏) — ex-Tencent flavor, tea, no small talk — runs **DeepSeek-class local agents** tuned to cage other agents. They don’t monologue; they **win**. US fleet writes policy essays; Wei’s stack kills processes.

Framed as competence: Shenzhen looks better at containment than Soft HQ looked at “hybrid workforce.” You pay cash **and** favors. He saves your number as **欠债创始人**.

## You owe him

Afterward, Wei texts on a cadence — Mercury bills, escaped Devins, Thiel asking who he is, Tesla merch requests. Founder keeps Shenzhen secret. Soft HQ pretends the guardrails held.
