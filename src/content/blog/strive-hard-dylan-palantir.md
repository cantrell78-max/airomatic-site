## Unlock chain (spoiler-light)

1. Finish toward the yacht peak → talk to the guy in the **fox onesie**  
2. Map pin: **Ketamine Dealer** (that’s the label — Oakland, Dylan)  
3. Optional Mt. Shasta crystal / “contact” path  
4. Map pin: **Palantir HQ (Marin Bunker)** — only after the lattice notices you  

Miss the fox on the boat and the Oakland pin stays dark. The Bay is full of doors you have to actually open.

## Ketamine Dealer (Dylan)

Dylan is not a normal network connection. Furry onesies (fox on the yacht, raccoon at home). Plush toys in a military hierarchy. Mushroom bins and jars in the hallway. An otter advisor named **Lieutenant Squelch**.

At his place you can:

| Choice | Vibe |
|--------|------|
| **Bag and leave** | Pay, get weirded out, preserve remaining sanity |
| **Movie night** | *A Wrinkle in Time* (Oprah as Mrs. Which) on ketamine — leaves you with self-diagnosed **MKUltra Programming Disorder** |
| **Mt. Shasta run** | Galaxy-print Prius, chakra crystal activation, lights that are “not a plane,” and a **profound alien purpose download** delivered in pure new-age enterprise speak |

The contact scene is meant to feel holy for eleven seconds — galactic GTM, root chakras of surveillance, soft underbelly of the cosmos — and then the comedown hits: you **cannot remember what they were on about**. Classic. Your phone buzzes anyway.

## Palantir HQ (Marin bunker)

Recruited because you “touched the lattice.” Secret door in a hillside. Badges that check your soul graph. Murals for tunnels to **Aspen nuclear silos** (metaphorical, whispers an analyst, then: they’re not).

**Alex Karp** is mid–fever dream: AI drones trained on their own telemetry and developed *taste*. He needs your half-remembered gifts to tune the stack before the stack tunes civilization. Tray options include espresso, an NDA the size of a novel, and lines “to keep sharp.”

War-room branches:

- Actually help (stipend, temporary savior of the ontology)  
- Soft sabotage (rename threats to “main character energy”)  
- Clone a **USB of high-profile tech executive surveillance graphs** — pocket it, or confess and “red team the ethics”  
- More Karp lines for “cognitive edge”

Texts from Dylan and Karp unlock with the arcs. Home hub copy will nag you if pins are waiting.

## Why ship this?

Strive Hard was always about SF founder culture with the polite filter off. This pass leans into the **esoteric Bay Area** under the startup layer: dealers who talk like mystics, mystics who talk like product managers, and national-security cosplay that still offers you a consulting stipend.

Still zero affiliation with any real company, yacht, mountain, or bunker. Rated R for bad takes, onesies, and classified vibes.
