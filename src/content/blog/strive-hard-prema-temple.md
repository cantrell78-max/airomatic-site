## Why this chapter exists

SF founder anxiety is a specific genre: everyone else announced a round, your wire is fog, LPs want chemistry meetings, and the algorithm keeps showing you champagne emojis that aren’t yours. Detachment is hard when your identity *is* the deck.

Prema doesn’t sell you a growth framework. He sells the opposite: **stay detached from the results of fruitive labor.** The labor is yours. The fruit was never the point — though the Bay will swear otherwise. Catch the carrot and… oh boy. You have a carrot. One man’s success is another’s degradation. Especially *these* carrots.

Funny. True enough to sting. Uplifting if you let it.

## What happens at the temple

Arrive → feast or darshan → **Swami Prema Das**. Full carrot monologue. Subtle karmi-past SF-scene innuendo (term sheet for love, chemistry meetings, sauna heat that isn’t spiritual — recovering, not recruiting). Choices: sit with it, argue that LPs need the carrot, ask for a fundraising mantra, or **seva** (dishes; more honest than most decks).

He ends up in your **texts**. Odd-day affirmations. Return visits are shorter check-ins: still mid-carrot?
