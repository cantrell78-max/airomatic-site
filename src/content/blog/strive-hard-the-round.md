## What’s in the chapter

### Spaces boss fight
Skylar goes live: *From Seed to Series (Delusion Optional)*. Perform founder mode, go chaotic mattress-true, or let Skylar carry while you nod. Priya may be in the room as friendly opposition. Garry lurks under a burner that is not subtle.

### Flaky term sheet (Thiel orbit)
DocuSign at 1:14 a.m. Valuation “TBD pending chemistry.” Observer rights that feel like hands. Open-kimono information rights. A side letter about deeper engagement and dinners without associates. Garry: *standard, also flaky — negotiate the exclusivity of the deal.*

### Data room dungeon
Folders including **05_Sensitive** and the cursed **06_Founder_Personal**. Clean docs, overshare, or — if you still have the stick from [the Palantir bunker](/blog/strive-hard-dylan-palantir/) — **USB mid-diligence**. Thiel and Karp both have opinions about that last one.

### FlareUp B-plot
Diligence does not pause attachment styles. Show up for the human, ghost with a founder essay, or open the app for five minutes of chaos before board prep.

### Board meeting
Zoom mosaic: **Peter Thiel**, **Garry Chan**, a normie LP who only says “unit economics,” **Mom** (“I Googled Series A”), Avery taking notes that will outlive you. If you did the bunker, Thiel heard about your *performance* from Karp’s people.

### Celebration that is a crisis
Champagne, dead fiddle-leaf, fraud alerts on the CELEBRATE virtual card, timeline weather, Mom’s cousin still has benefits. Put out fires with ops, post through it, or accept Garry’s sauna debrief.
