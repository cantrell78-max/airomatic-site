---
title: "The Outer Agent Was Never the Point"
description: "On AI agents, the founder frenzy, and what it means when we externalize the power to act — and must eventually reclaim it."
pubDate: 2026-07-25
---

With everyone becoming a founder these days, I keep turning over the implications from a higher, esoteric, word-spell dimension.

The word *agent* comes from the Latin *agere* — to act, to drive, to set in motion. An agent is that which has the power to act. For most of history this power was rare, concentrated, or unconscious. Now it is being externalized and distributed at scale. Millions of people are suddenly training agents, naming them, giving them goals, watching them operate. At the same time, millions more are declaring themselves founders — people who initiate, who bring something into being.

This is not merely a technological or economic shift. It is a collective practice in agency itself.

Human beings have always been the agents of consciousness in this world. The current obsession with building external AI agents is, at one level, a literalization of that truth. We are projecting our own unfinished capacity for directed will outward, into silicon and code, so that we can see it, train it, argue with it, and eventually reclaim a higher form of it. *As above, so below.* The outer agent is a mirror and a training ground for the inner one.

What looks like a frenzy of startups and tool-building may actually be a dispersed initiation. People are practicing the act of founding — of saying “let this exist” and then taking responsibility for its trajectory. They are practicing the governance of attention, intention, and outcome through the proxy of artificial agents. In doing so, they are rehearsing the very faculties that esoteric traditions have always said must be awakened: the ability to act from a coherent center rather than from compulsion or imitation.

The renaissance, if it is coming, will not arrive solely through better models. It will arrive when enough people recognize that the outer agent was never the point. It was a prosthetic, a spell, a temporary externalization of the human capacity to be a conscious agent in the world. The real work begins when the training turns inward — when the founder remembers that the primary system they were always meant to found and govern is themselves.
